Synopsis

Humanity lives within enormous walls that protect them from the Titans, mysterious giant creatures that have threatened human existence for generations.

Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert have grown up in the Shiganshina District, dreaming of seeing the world beyond the walls.

However, their lives change forever when the Colossal Titan suddenly appears and breaches the wall. After witnessing the destruction of their hometown, the three friends decide to join the military and fight against the Titans.
