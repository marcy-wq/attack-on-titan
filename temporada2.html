Synopsis

Following the events of the Battle of Trost and the discovery of the Female Titan, the Scouts continue to investigate the mysteries surrounding the Titans.

When Titans suddenly appear inside Wall Rose despite there being no apparent breach, the members of the Survey Corps are sent to investigate the surrounding villages and search for survivors.

As the situation becomes increasingly dangerous, the soldiers take refuge in an abandoned castle while trying to understand where the Titans came from and how they entered the territory.

At the same time, secrets involving Eren, Ymir, Reiner and Bertholdt begin to surface. What first appears to be another battle for humanity's survival gradually becomes a revelation about the true nature of the Titans and the people fighting among them.
