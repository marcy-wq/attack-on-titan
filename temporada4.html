Original Work Hajime Isayama's manga

Synopsis

Four years after the events of Shiganshina, the story moves beyond the Walls and introduces Marley and its Warrior candidates. Eren and the people of Paradis are no longer fighting an enemy they know only as Titans; they are now facing a much larger world and a conflict shaped by years of hatred and war.

As Eren's actions become increasingly independent, tensions grow between him, Mikasa, Armin and the Survey Corps. At the same time, Zeke develops his own plan involving Eldians, Titans and the future of Paradis.

The conflict eventually reaches a point of no return. The Rumbling begins, sending countless Wall Titans across the world while the former enemies of Paradis are forced to consider working together.

With the fate of humanity at stake, the remaining characters must put aside years of conflict and make difficult choices in an attempt to stop Eren. The final confrontation brings the story of humanity, freedom, war and the Titans to its conclusion.
