ARC 07

Return to Shiganshina

The battle to reclaim humanity's lost home.

Overview

After uncovering the secrets surrounding the Royal Government, the Survey Corps prepares for a dangerous mission to reclaim Wall Maria and return to Shiganshina District. Their objective is to seal the breach that allowed the Titans to invade years earlier and reach the basement of Eren Yeager's childhood home, where answers about the world may finally be revealed. Upon arriving in Shiganshina, the soldiers discover that their enemies have anticipated their movements. The mission quickly turns into a desperate battle as the Survey Corps faces the Armored Titan, the Colossal Titan, and the Beast Titan. Forced to make difficult decisions, the soldiers must rely on strategy, courage, and sacrifice to stand a chance of succeeding.

As the battle reaches its conclusion, the survivors face choices that will shape humanity's future. The long-awaited secrets hidden in Eren's basement promise to challenge everything they thought they knew about the Titans, the walls, and the world beyond them.
