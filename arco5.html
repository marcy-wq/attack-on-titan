ARC 05

Clash of the Titans

When the truth begins to unravel.

Overview

Following the events in Stohess, humanity faces a new series of mysteries when Titans suddenly appear within Wall Rose. With no clear explanation for how they entered the territory, the soldiers race to warn nearby settlements and investigate the threat before more lives are lost. As the situation grows more desperate, members of the 104th Cadet Corps become involved in a dangerous mission that reveals unexpected connections between some of their former comrades and the Titans. Meanwhile, the Survey Corps struggles to understand the secrets surrounding the walls and the true nature of their enemies.

The arc explores themes of betrayal, loyalty, and the difficult choices faced by soldiers during wartime. As shocking revelations emerge, Eren and his allies must confront threats that challenge everything they believed about humanity's struggle for survival.
