ARC 04

Female Titan

A mysterious enemy hiding among the ranks.

Overview

Following their graduation, Eren Yeager and his fellow cadets begin their first mission with the Survey Corps, humanity's military branch responsible for exploring the territory beyond the walls. Under the command of Erwin Smith, the expedition sets out into Titan territory with the goal of advancing humanity's understanding of its enemies. During the expedition, the soldiers encounter an intelligent and unusually powerful Female Titan capable of running, fighting strategically, and targeting specific members of the Survey Corps. Her appearance throws the formation into chaos and forces the soldiers to reconsider what they know about the Titans.

As the expedition unfolds, Eren and his comrades become involved in a dangerous attempt to capture the mysterious Titan and uncover its identity. The mission tests the Survey Corps' discipline, loyalty, and willingness to make difficult sacrifices in pursuit of answers.
