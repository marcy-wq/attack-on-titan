ARC 02

104th Cadet Corps

The making of humanity's next generation of soldiers.

Overview

The 104th Cadet Corps arc focuses on the training and development of the young recruits who will become humanity's next generation of soldiers. Determined to fight the Titans and protect what remains of human civilization, Eren Yeager joins the military alongside his childhood friends Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert. Throughout their training, the cadets learn essential combat techniques, including the use of Vertical Maneuvering Equipment, while facing demanding physical and mental challenges. Under the guidance of their instructors, they develop their abilities, discover their strengths and weaknesses, and begin forming important friendships and rivalries.

As graduation approaches, the cadets must decide which military branch they wish to join: the Survey Corps, the Garrison, or the Military Police Brigade. Their experiences during training shape their motivations and prepare them for the difficult choices and dangers that await beyond the training grounds.
