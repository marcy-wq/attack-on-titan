ARC 06

Royal Government

The secrets hidden behind the walls.

Overview

Following the revelations of the previous arc, the Survey Corps begins investigating the secrets hidden within the walls and the true identity of Historia Reiss. However, their search for answers puts them in direct conflict with the Royal Government, which seeks to maintain its authority and prevent certain truths from coming to light. As the military police pursue Eren Yeager and Historia, Captain Levi and his squad are forced to operate outside the usual military structure. Meanwhile, Erwin Smith develops a plan that could expose the corruption within the government and change the future of humanity inside the walls.

The arc shifts the focus from fighting Titans to political conspiracies, hidden identities, and the struggle for power. Through dangerous confrontations and unexpected alliances, the Survey Corps must uncover the truth behind the monarchy and decide how far they are willing to go to protect humanity's future.
