About the Manga

Attack on Titan, also known as Shingeki no Kyojin, is a Japanese manga written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. It takes place in a world where humanity lives behind enormous walls to protect itself from giant creatures known as Titans. The story follows Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert as their lives change after their hometown is attacked.

Combining action, mystery and political conflict, the manga gradually expands its world and explores themes such as freedom, survival, war and the consequences of hatred.

About the Author

Hajime Isayama was born on August 29, 1986, in Ōyama, Ōita, Japan. He became interested in drawing manga while growing up and submitted an early version of Attack on Titan to a Kodansha newcomer award in 2006, where it received recognition. The initial idea was developed into a serialized story, which began in the monthly magazine Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine in September 2009.

Instead of publishing the entire story at once, Isayama released it chapter by chapter in the magazine. These chapters were later collected into individual volumes. The series ran for more than eleven years, ending in April 2021 with chapter 139. The final collected volume was published in Japan in June of that year.
