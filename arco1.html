ARC 01

Fall of Shiganshina

The beginning of humanity's fight for survival.

Overview

The Fall of Shiganshina marks the beginning of Attack on Titan. Humanity lives surrounded by enormous walls designed to protect people from the Titans, mysterious creatures that devour humans. Eren Yeager dreams of exploring the world beyond the walls, while his friend Armin Arlert shares his curiosity about the outside world. Mikasa Ackerman, who lives with Eren's family, tries to keep him safe.

However, their lives change completely when a Colossal Titan appears outside Wall Maria, followed by an attack that destroys the district of Shiganshina and forces its inhabitants to flee. This event becomes the starting point of Eren's journey and humanity's struggle against the Titans.
