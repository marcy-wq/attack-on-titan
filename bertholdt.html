Bertholdt Hoover

Bertholdt Hoover is a former member of the 104th Cadet Corps. Quiet and reserved, he rarely draws attention to himself and prefers to observe situations before acting. Despite his calm demeanor, he possesses remarkable abilities and demonstrates determination when carrying out his responsibilities.

Bertholdt was described by others as a weak-willed and quiet person. This drastically changed when he became fully committed to his duty during the Battle of Shiganshina.
