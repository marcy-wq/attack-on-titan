Hange Zoe

Hange Zoe is an officer of the Survey Corps and one of the organization's leading researchers. Fascinated by Titans, Hange dedicates extensive effort to studying their nature and behavior. Their intelligence, curiosity, and innovative approach have greatly contributed to humanity's understanding of its greatest threat.
