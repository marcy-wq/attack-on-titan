Synopsis

After the events of the previous season, the Survey Corps faces a new threat that does not come from the Titans alone. Eren and Historia become targets of the government, forcing Levi and his soldiers to protect them while uncovering the truth behind the royal family.

The conflict gradually moves into the political world within the Walls. As the Scouts discover more about Historia's family and the true history of the kingdom, they realize that the government has hidden crucial information from the people for generations.

After Historia becomes queen, the Survey Corps prepares for its most important mission yet: returning to Shiganshina and reaching the basement of Eren's childhood home.

The second half of the season brings the Scouts face to face with Reiner, Bertholdt and Zeke. The battle for Wall Maria becomes a desperate struggle that finally reveals the truth about the world beyond the Walls.
