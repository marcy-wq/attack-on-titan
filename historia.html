Historia Reiss

Historia Reiss is a member of the Survey Corps known for her kindness, compassion, and sense of responsibility. Although gentle in nature, she possesses remarkable inner strength and gradually grows into a confident and dependable leader. Her presence plays an important role within the Walls.

During her cadet days, she went by the name of Christa Len to take on a new identity, after being coerced into renouncing her name and claim to the Reiss heritage. She eventually reclaimed her true name, becoming an important asset to the Regiment.
