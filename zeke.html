Zeke Yeager

Zeke Yeager is a high-ranking Warrior from Marley and a brilliant military strategist. Known for his calm demeanor, analytical thinking, and leadership abilities, he plays a significant role in the conflict between nations. His ideals and long-term objectives make him one of the series' most influential figures.
