ARC 09

War for Paradis

The struggle for freedom and the fate of the world.

Overview

Following the events in Marley, the conflict between the nations reaches a turning point as Eren Yeager carries out a plan that changes the course of the war. Back on Paradis Island, the military faces growing divisions, and the relationships between former allies become increasingly strained as different groups struggle to decide the island's future. As the situation escalates, the Survey Corps and the remaining military forces become caught between competing ideologies and conflicting visions of freedom. Old friendships are tested, unexpected alliances emerge, and the characters must confront the consequences of their past decisions while trying to protect the people they care about.

With the fate of the world at stake, the conflict expands beyond the walls and forces former enemies to reconsider what they are fighting for. The arc brings together the story's central themes of freedom, hatred, sacrifice, and the cycle of violence, leading toward the final confrontation that will determine humanity's future.
